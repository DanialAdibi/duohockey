Duo Hockey privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

In short: Duo Hockey doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, match statistics, records, XP, level, achievements and CPU Tour stars are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game reports your achievements and leaderboard scores (for example your fastest shot) to Game Center. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your Apple Account details. You can turn Game Center off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Purchases

The optional one-off "Unlock everything" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The game only checks with Apple whether the unlock has been bought on your Apple Account.

Sensors

On iPhone Duo the game reads the hinge angle to tilt the table. This reading is used only while you play and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Children

Duo Hockey is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
