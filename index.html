Duo Hockey

Air hockey for iPhone Duo. Fold the phone to tilt the table, and play face to face on one screen.

Need help? Email danialadibi@icloud.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I restore my unlock on a new phone?
Open the Table screen (or the unlock screen) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

The table doesn't tilt when I fold my phone.
Open Table and check that Follow the hinge is on. Tour opponents play on their own fixed table, so the hinge is ignored in Tour matches.

I can't hear any sound.
The game respects your silent switch. Turn silent mode off and check the volume.

Do I need an iPhone Duo?
The game is designed for iPhone Duo. On other iPhones you can set the fold angle with a slider instead.

What does the unlock include?
Multi-puck and Timed modes, the Hard CPU, CPU Tour opponents 4 to 10, and power-ups. It's a single payment with no subscription and no ads.
